# Revival vision

Revival is a **source-backed custom Bible compiler**.

Its purpose is not to produce one preferred translation. Its purpose is to let people explore Scripture through chosen linguistic and experiential projections while keeping a replayable path back to the source-backed kernel and every consequential choice made along the way.

## Bible as curiosity engine

The Bible is not only a fixed presentation to consume. It can be entered through many doors:

- language;
- morphology and syntax;
- characters and relationships;
- speech and dialogue;
- places and movement;
- objects and material culture;
- chronology;
- music and sound;
- visual structure;
- games and playable encounters;
- reconstructed rooms, roads, households, cities, wildernesses, courts, temples, seas, and empires.

A projection may be scholarly, readable, strange, entertaining, visual, sonic, or inhabitable.

The rule is not **stay close in appearance**.

The rule is **keep the crossing attributable**.

## Worlds within the world

A future Revival surface might let someone:

- compile a rigid source-order interlinear;
- choose a particular transliteration convention;
- prefer names or titles rendered in a chosen way;
- preserve grammatical distinctions that ordinary English flattens;
- compile a children's exploratory view;
- follow every scene involving a place, object, person, or relation;
- enter a reconstructed household and inspect why an object or action is associated with a passage;
- move from a playable scene back to its verse, linguistic structure, evidence, and unresolved disagreement;
- generate a musical or phonographic projection whose derivation remains inspectable.

Those outputs are not the witness.

They are lawful descendants with visible ancestry.

## Provenance is permission to go farther

Revival's provenance machinery exists so experimentation can become **more adventurous**, not more timid.

```text
source-backed kernel
        ↓
declared linguistic / interpretive material
        ↓
projection recipe
        ↓
readable / scholarly / visual / sonic / playable world
        ↓
trace + delta + receipt
```

Curiosity chooses the door.

The receipt keeps the door from becoming a false wall.

## Relationship to canon

Revival does not establish biblical canon or textual-critical priority. It does not promote a projection, annotation, translation decision, reconstruction, game state, or interpretation into source authority.

It preserves the distinctions needed for those things to coexist without impersonating one another.

## Current executable frontier

Revival 002 proves only the first user-facing slice:

**one held Genesis 1:1 witness → source-anchored tokens → declared linguistic recipe → compiled text → token-level trace → delta → receipt.**

That is deliberately smaller than the vision.

The next useful frontier is to replace hand-authored demonstration annotations with imported, attributable linguistic layers from real source-backed corpora and then allow the same recipe mechanism to compile larger passages.
