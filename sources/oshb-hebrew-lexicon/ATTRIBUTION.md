# Open Scriptures Hebrew Lexicon attribution

Revival 012 uses a small curated proof extract aligned to the **Open Scriptures
Hebrew Lexicon** repository.

Pinned upstream:

- repository: `openscriptures/HebrewLexicon`
- commit: `21c9add13bc727d3a951361778e97e3ff7afd1ce`

Open Scriptures documents the project files under **CC BY 4.0** and identifies
the underlying Strong's Hebrew Dictionary text as public domain. Attribution:
**Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible Project**.

The checked-in JSON is deliberately a small proof extract rather than a
replacement lexicon. It records the exact upstream identities needed by the
Genesis 1:1-3 experiment.

## Boundary

The extract preserves lexical identity and a compact lexical range.

It does not assert that:

- a lexeme has only one sense;
- two occurrences of one lexeme have the same referent;
- a noun is a person, place, or physical object;
- a lexical gloss is a translation verdict;
- lexical identity establishes theology, geography, or historical
  reconstruction.

Revival first aligns the OSHB lemma and morphology morphemes. Only then may the
corresponding augmented Strong component open a lexeme door.
